# groww × navya

Navya Juneja's product portfolio for Groww PM roles, built to feel like browsing an investing app.

- **Explore**: index cards, "most viewed" cases, products & tools, top movers (filter by evidence), a follow-the-money return calculator.
- **Screener** (`#/cases`): every case study, filterable by category, company and evidence label.
- **Stock pages** (`#/stock/<id>`): headline value, Overview / Approach / Media / Why Groww / Peers, and a Hire / Refer order panel.
- **PhonePe Fund** (`#/fund`): the two years at PhonePe as a fund, with its holdings and managers.
- **Holdings** (`#/holdings`): experience and education. **Watchlist** is saved in the browser only.

Every figure is labelled Result, Built, Modelled, Target or Sourced. Charts are decorative.

Static site: open `index.html` or serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`). Content lives in `assets/data.js`.

Independent job-application portfolio. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Groww or PhonePe.
